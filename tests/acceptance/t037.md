## Deterministic contract

Initial eligible VIOLATED plus independent fresh eligible VIOLATED yields
CONFIRMED. Applicable independent SATISFIED yields NON-REPRODUCED. Missing,
ineligible, NOT APPLICABLE or UNEVALUABLE verification yields INCONCLUSIVE.
Execution failures remain run/trial outcomes and do not invent predicate verdicts.
Budget admission, reset, baseline and cancellation failures cannot manufacture
confirmation or non-reproduction.

Predicate ID, version and canonical hash must match exactly, with immutable
provenance. Edited versions and late approvals cannot reuse old evidence. Replay
requires independent trial, baseline and observation references, validated reset
and baseline execution, and the frozen journey. Discovery cannot confirm itself.
Unapproved hypotheses cannot become confirmed by repeated violations.

Later contradictory independent evidence appends a transition, retains previous
evidence and historical classifications, and marks consistency false with current
INCONCLUSIVE state. Consistency cannot silently become true again. Unrelated later
execution failure preserves a previously established finding without fabricating
evidence. Order-specific semantics remain in fixture/predicate test data.
